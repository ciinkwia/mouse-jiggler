MOUSE JIGGLER
=============
Keeps your PC awake and your status "active".

HOW TO USE
1. Double-click MouseJiggler.exe. It starts ON.
2. When you stop using the mouse/keyboard, it waits a random 8-45 seconds
   (you can change this), then glides the mouse to a random nearby spot like a
   person would. If you grab the mouse, it gets out of your way.
3. It also stops the PC from sleeping or turning off the screen while ON.
4. Click the big button to turn it off/on. Minimize it and it stays on your
   taskbar like any other app.
5. Close the window (the X) to quit.

CAN'T FIND IT? Press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager, find
"MouseJiggler", click it, then click "End task".

NO INSTALL. No internet. Works on Windows 10 and 11.

"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" WARNING?
That shows up for any small program that isn't from a big company.
Click "More info" then "Run anyway".

Source code is in Jiggler.cs if you want to see exactly what it does.
